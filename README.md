## How I build

- *Count what is real.* Order and revenue numbers only include verified payments. A dashboard that flatters the business is worse than no dashboard.
- *Money and attribution first.* If you cannot tell who earned what, and pay them correctly, the product is a demo.
- *Boring technology on purpose.* A small team has to operate what it ships.
- *Ship, then watch it.* Order states, payout runs and failure paths are instrumented from day one.
